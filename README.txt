Files
- index.html: complete responsive landing page with embedded CSS and JavaScript
- preview.png: full desktop-page preview
- mobile-preview.png: full mobile-page preview

Design direction
- Restrained, journal-first visual system inspired by the clarity and hierarchy of JAMA Cardiology
- Centered masthead, compact issue navigation, strong serif headlines, thin rules, and minimal shadows
- Featured research with New Online and Most Read modules
- Dedicated AJPC Conversations section for author and editor interviews
- Dedicated AJPC Career Center for faculty, research, clinical, fellowship, and industry postings
- Topics, clinical briefing, author submission, and newsletter modules

Notes
- Article titles, author names, interview subjects, institutions, and job listings are demonstration content.
- Navigation targets and forms are non-functional placeholders.
- The AJPC wordmark shown here is a design concept and should be replaced with approved journal branding.
