Developing the next generation

AJPC Academy

The AJPC Academy is an early-career–driven initiative that develops future leaders in preventive cardiology through mentorship and hands-on academic experience.

Visit the official Academy page ↗
Learn by contributing

Inside the Academy

01

Mentored peer review

Develop rigorous manuscript assessment skills with guidance from experienced journal leaders.

02

Scientific dissemination

Help translate preventive cardiology research into accessible, timely communication.

03

Editorial experience

Gain practical exposure to scholarly publishing and the work of an academic journal.

04

Public engagement

Extend the journal’s reach through social media, press releases, and author interviews.

As part of AJPC’s broader mission, the Academy brings together trainees and junior faculty in a collaborative global community committed to translating cardiovascular-prevention evidence into impact.
